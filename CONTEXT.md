# Campaign preparation

Language for turning an adventure source into material a GM can use and revise.

## Language

**Source**:
A book, PDF, note, or testimony whose content can be cited but does not establish what happened in this campaign.

**Preparation document**:
A private, editable GM sheet about a person, place, faction, situation, or other play material. It may be based on a source or invented, and remains a proposal until the GM uses or accepts it.
_Avoid_: Extract, PDF card

**Relation**:
A directed connection between preparation subjects that names their interaction or motive, such as "Voz employs Dmiri." It can change as play changes the subjects.

**Scenario chronology**:
The source's past, expected sequence, and conditional future, labeled as preparation rather than events that occurred at the table.
_Avoid_: Campaign history

**Campaign history**:
Events observed in play and interpreted through the GM's decisions. It may diverge from the scenario chronology.

**Canon**:
An assertion the GM has established for this campaign through a decision; a source or preparation document never becomes canon by being imported.
